Field rule

Keep your GPS switched on whenever you are in the field.

Do not edit the GPS
  • Do not delete any GPS points.
  • Do not manually rename any GPS points.
Correct order

Download the GPS first. Enter your data after that, so observations can be matched to the right track data.
